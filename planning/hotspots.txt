 converted each crash's latitude and longitude into a projected coordinate system (NY State Plane, in feet). I then ran DBSCAN clustering, which groups crashes that lie within 300 feet of each other and requires at least 15 crashes to form a cluster. Crashes that didn't meet this were labeled noise. Each resulting cluster is a hotspot. For each one I summed crashes, injuries, deaths, and a weighted severity score, and labeled it with its most common street names. Hotspots were ranked by severity.
